Verdict

The 5090 decodes this model about thirty times faster, plain to plain. But the same abliterated 27B — the one the post wraps in a custom CUDA runtime and a $2,000 card — runs all day on a tablet’s integrated GPU, faster than anyone reads, and the post’s own speculative-draft trick still buys +19.5% for free. The 5090 is for bragging. The model runs anywhere with 17 GB of memory. Our context is configured at 32K, not the post’s 256K, and we ran zero dollars of cloud to find out.Pending measurements.
